Safety Concept Summary#

The Health Monitor is an in-process library that supervises the application it is linked into. It detects deviations from the configured temporal and logical behavior and reacts by withholding the alive notification towards the Launch Manager.

  • Safe state: the state the system reaches through the Recovery Action of the Launch Manager. The Health Monitor triggers it by stopping its alive notifications; the Launch Manager detects the missing notifications via its alive supervision.

  • Fail-silent principle: any failure, whether detected in the application or internal to the Health Monitor (crash, hang, panic, starvation), stops the alive notifications and thereby leads to the safe state. A fault can therefore never make the Health Monitor report “alive” for a failed application.

  • Detection channel: the Launch Manager alive supervision is the independent channel that covers failures of the Health Monitor itself.
